ViewPoint is peer reviewed by ambulatory care nursing experts and indexed in the Cumulative Index of Nursing and Allied Health Literature (CINAHL). It is distributed to members as a direct benefit of membership.

ViewPointhas been vetted by the International Academy of Nursing Editors (INANE) and is included in the INANE Nursing Journal Directory.

Nursing Continuing Professional Development

Free Nursing Continuing Education (NCPD) credit from ViewPoint articles is FREE to members. Non-members may purchase the articles (in pdf format) and CNE from the AAACN Online Library.
