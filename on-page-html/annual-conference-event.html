Join US in New Orleans!

Dates: April 27-30, 2027
Location:New Orleans Marriott, New Orleans, LA

As the ONLY national conference dedicated to ambulatory care nursing, AAACN's Annual Conference is designed to support both your personal and professional development.

Network with peers, explore the latest advances in ambulatory care nursing, and attend sessions on topics such as care coordination, telehealth innovations, patient engagement strategies, and evidence-based practice.

Earn continuing education credits, work toward certification, gain practical insights to improve your daily practice, and have a whole lot of fun too!

The four-day schedule features a pre-conference workshop as well as a post-conference Ambulatory Care Nursing Certification Review Course.

What to Expect

  • Inspiring keynote speakers
  • Live presentations
  • Exhibit hall
  • Poster presentations
  • Wellness activities
  • Awards ceremony
  • Unparalleled networking opportunities
  • On-demand options to meet your needs if you are unable to join us in person
