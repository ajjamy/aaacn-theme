AAACN Memberships for Individuals

Active RN - $199/year

For registered nurses who are actively practicing in ambulatory care nursing or related fields.

Affiliate - $175/year

For individuals with a professional interest in ambulatory care who are not registered nurses, including LPNs, LVN's and Medical Assistants

Student - $99/year

For students currently enrolled in a professional nursing program, studying toward initial RN licensure, with an interest in ambulatory care nursing.

Senior - $99/year

For individuals who are 62 years of age and have been an ACTIVE member for at least 3 years prior to reaching age 62.
