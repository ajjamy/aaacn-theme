Eligibility

If you work in any of these roles or settings, you are eligible for AAACN!

Ambulatory Care Nursing Roles

  • Administrators and directors
  • Advanced practice nurses
  • Care coordinators
  • Consultants
  • Educators
  • Hospital-based outpatient clinics/centers
  • Managers and supervisors
  • Researchers
  • Staff nurses

Ambulatory Care Nursing Settings

  • Colleges/educational institutions
  • Community hospitals
  • Free standing facilities
  • Military and VA settings
  • Managed care/HMOs/PPOs
  • Patient homes
  • Solo/Group medical practices
  • Telehealth call centers
  • University hospitals

Benefits of Membership

  • Free monthly ANCC contact hours
  • Free copy of AAACN's Journal of Ambulatory Care Nursing
  • Take advantage of the monthly Membership Savings Program
  • $100 discount on ANCC's Ambulatory Care Nursing Certification
  • AAACN's scholarships, research grants, awards, and ViewPoint publication
  • Access to AAACN's Special Interest Groups (SIGs)
  • Networking opportunites in members-only online communities
  • Monthly Email Newsletter
  • Opportunities to serve on task forces, committees, SIGs, and more
  • Ability to run for office, serve on AAACN's committees, and vote for leadership (Active, Lifetime, and Senior Members only)
  • Resources to help you stay up to date on ambulatory care nursing, care coordination and transition management, and telehealth nursing

Discounts on:

  • AAACN resources, publications, and courses
  • $100 off ANCC's Ambulatory Care Nursing Certification
  • Discounted registration for AAACN's Annual Conference
  • Travel and ticket discounts through MemberDeals
  • Choice of discounted subscription to one of three key nursing journals:
    • Nursing Economic$
    • MEDSURG Nursing
    • Pediatric Nursing
