The American Academy of Ambulatory Care Nursing (AAACN) is the premier professional organization for practicing nurses and nurse leaders specialized in serving people in community and ambulatory care settings.

Dedicated to Shaping Care Where Life Happens, AAACN advances holistic and evidence-based care and advocates for the nurses' essential role in improving the health of our nation.

AAACN was founded in 1978 and is a 501(c)(3) not-for-profit New Jersey corporation.

What is Ambulatory Care Nursing?

Ambulatory care nursing is a specialized field focused on providing care in outpatient settings, supporting individuals, families, and communities across all stages of life.

Healthcare is undergoing radical change and transformation. With a majority of healthcare moving to the outpatient and virtual settings, ambulatory care nurses play a critical role in primary and specialty care, ensuring patients receive high-quality, coordinated support without hospitalization. Their expertise is crucial in reducing hospital admissions, enhancing efficiency, and improving the overall healthcare experience.
