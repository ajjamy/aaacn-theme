What is Ambulatory Care Nursing?

Ambulatory care nursing is a dynamic role dedicated to delivering high-quality healthcare in outpatient settings. Ambulatory care nurses support individuals, families, and communities throughout every stage of life, providing coordinated, patient-centered care outside of the hospital environment. Ambulatory care nurses play a vital role in helping patients achieve the best possible health outcomes.

We are shaping care where life happens.
