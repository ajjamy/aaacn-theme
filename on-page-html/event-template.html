Interacting with Federal Agents: Putting Patients First

Wednesday, September 9, 2026
7:00 PM ET

1.0 ANCC NCPD contact hour

This webinar will discuss what recent policy changes mean for healthcare environments, clarify the rights patients and providers maintain, and outline practical ways to reduce risk through clear communication, thoughtful planning, and staff training. Participants will leave with actionable steps to support patients, strengthen team readiness, and uphold safe, ethical care.

Learning Outcome

After completing this learning activity, the participant will be able to understand the effects of increased federal activities, the rights we have as healthcare providers, and make a plan for training into action.
