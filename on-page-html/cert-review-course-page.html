Reinforce Your Knowledge, Prepare for Certification

The American Academy of Ambulatory Care Nursing (AAACN) Certification Review Course (CRC) will help you prepare to take the Ambulatory Care Nursing Certification Exam.

The CRC will reinforce your knowledge of ambulatory care, identify your weak areas, and help you learn new information. All topics are offered in an interactive, case problem-solving style to enhance critical thinking.

Course content is based on the Test Content Outline for the Ambulatory Care Nursing Certification Exam.

The course provides an overview of the potential content that may be tested on the Ambulatory Care Nursing Certification Exam. Note: This course does NOT grant certification in ambulatory care nursing.

On the Road Ambulatory Care Nursing Certification Review Course

AAACN's “On the Road” Ambulatory Care Nursing Certification Review Course is a one-day, in-person course (8.0 contact hours) based on the American Nurses Credentialing Center (ANCC) Test Content Outline for the Ambulatory Care Nursing Certification (AMB-BC™) exam. The AAACN On the Road Course allows your organization to host the Certification Review Course at your facility with our trained instructors providing the course content. The target audience for this course includes:

  • RNs preparing for the AMB-BC™ exam

  • Nurses transitioning into ambulatory care

  • Nurses seeking a refresher in the ambulatory care nursing specialty

This course reinforces ambulatory care knowledge, helps identify areas needing further study, and provides the latest information in the specialty. Please note: This course does not grant certification in ambulatory care nursing.
